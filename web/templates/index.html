About the Forever roster

Based on Blizzard's race and class table. Forever is in beta, with launch scheduled for November 4.

Nine races, nine classes. Skyborne choose a faction at character creation: High Order can be Mages; Windshapers can be Shaman. Both can be Warriors, Hunters, Rogues, and Druids. The Skyborne race requires an upgrade pack.

“New in Forever” marks the new Classic-era combinations. Race and class are optional here; choosing a new race clears a class it cannot play.

How to read this audit

Jev evaluates all questions in one request. Scores are weighted positions on five-level rubrics; the shown label is the most likely level. Grade and style are its chosen options, including tied choices.

Mean certainty averages the six score and two choice confidences. It is not the probability this audit is correct. Name signals estimate a yes answer; only Blizzard decides naming-policy enforcement and availability.
